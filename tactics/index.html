TWELVE HEROES OF LIGHT

Squire, Lancer, Crossbowwoman, Acolyte, Warrior, Archer, Mage, Scout, Knight, Paladin, Priestess and Archmage, each with a role and an ability.

MERGE OR WAIT

Collect three copies and merge them into a stronger unit with a new ability… or keep all three on the field. Your call.

TEN LEVELS TO THE BOSS

Each run is ten levels in one biome, with elites along the way and, at the end, the horde with the boss: in the Forest, the Treant.

ESSENCE, FENCES AND AMULETS

Earn essence in every battle and spend it on fences that slow the horde and amulets for your heroes: more range for the archer, more power for the mage.
